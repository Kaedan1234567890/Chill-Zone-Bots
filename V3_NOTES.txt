Important identity limitation:
Carpet 26.2's createFake(String username, ...) itself chooses/resolves the UUID. If a real
Mojang profile exists for a chosen name, Carpet can resolve/fetch that profile. V3 therefore
uses uncommon names to reduce collision risk rather than pretending we can inject arbitrary
UUIDs through Carpet's public createFake method.
